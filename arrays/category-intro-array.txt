○ Neuro-cognitive dissonance of displaying an object much larger than it actually is. When the subject is a 35mm slide or instant film with a canonical size to which contains a "traditional " photographic subject also of known size, there is push-pull-push. This composite result seems quite different than a macro photo of a bug or a wide angle photo of an enormous landscape which are understood with cognitive ease.*
